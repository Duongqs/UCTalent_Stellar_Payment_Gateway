📖 Demo walkthrough (6 steps)
  1. Connect your Freighter wallet on Stellar testnet (SEP-10 auth).
  2. Employer: post a job and deposit into escrow — pay USDC directly, or send XLM and let Path Payment swap it to USDC at the best rate.
  3. Candidate: apply to the job and submit the milestone.
  4. Employer: approve the milestone to release the escrow deposit.
  5. Candidate: claim the payout and register a bank account with KYC (SEP-12 · SEP-9).
  6. Watch the SEP-31 anchor quote the rate (SEP-38) and settle to fiat via 9Pay.
